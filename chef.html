The Kitchen

Mastery, Authentic Spices & Culinary Passion

The dedicated team of chefs behind every dish at Prime Restaurant — committed to authentic Indian taste, hygiene, and generous hospitality.

Executive Chef

Chef & Kitchen Mastery

Our kitchen team brings decades of combined culinary expertise in authentic North-Indian, Punjabi, Tandoori, and Oriental cooking. Every dish is crafted from freshly ground spices, premium cottage cheese, and handpicked vegetables.

Whether preparing a rich Sp. Prime Paneer gravy, flaming a Chinese sizzler, or baking soft garlic naans in the clay tandoor, our chefs maintain uncompromised standards of taste and quality.
